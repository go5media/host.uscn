Privacy & Camera Operation

Operated by USCN. Managed with privacy in mind.

All public-safety cameras hosted through our network remain under USCN's ownership and operational control. We handle installation, configuration, maintenance and the secure management of recordings, so hosts are not responsible for operating the CCTV system.

Our cameras are installed to support public safety, with careful consideration given to their positioning, coverage and privacy impact. Recordings are normally retained for approximately three days, and cameras are not routinely monitored live.

Access to footage is restricted and managed by USCN. Hosting a camera does not provide automatic access to recordings, and police do not have direct access to our systems. Requests for footage are considered individually in accordance with our procedures and applicable law.
